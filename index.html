About Mr.Wesonga Timothy

Mr. Wesonga Timothy is an Information Scientist and Software Engineer specializing in Information Technology, holding a Bachelor of Science in Information Science (Information Technology option) from Rongo University. I have extensive experience in digital literacy, library information systems, and community-based ICT training. My work focuses on bridging the digital divide by leveraging technology to enhance information access, cybersecurity awareness, and inclusive digital education.

My professional journey began at Rongo University Library, where I supported students and faculty in accessing e-resources, research materials, and digital repositories. I then joined the Kenya National Library Service (KNLS) – Kibera Branch as an Information Technology Intern, conducting digital literacy training, supporting ICT skills development, and promoting assistive technology for persons with disabilities. Later, I worked at the Apollo Wakiaga Foundation Community Library, managing library operations, coordinating the Tent Reading Initiative and Book Box programs, and providing ICT training for students and community members in Nyamanga, Migori County.

In March 2024, I joined the Hope for Kenya Slum Adolescents Initiative (HKSAI) as the IT/Public Relations and Communication Officer, where I support the organization’s digital systems, lead communication and public relations strategies, conduct ICT and computer operations training at the HKSAI Training Centre, and facilitate community outreach programs to empower adolescents in informal settlements.

As a Software Engineer, I have hands-on experience in Python, PostgreSQL, Django/Django REST Framework, and RESTful API development. I build clean, responsive interfaces and scalable software solutions across both frontend and backend systems. I have designed relational databases, written optimized queries, implemented secure API integrations, and developed robust backend services. I have also applied my skills to community-focused projects addressing governance, democracy, climate change, and behavior change, combining technical proficiency with a strong problem-solving and user-centered approach.

I am proactive, detail-oriented, and thrive in collaborative environments. Across my roles in library science, ICT, community development, and software engineering, I remain committed to technology-driven education, digital inclusion, and innovative solutions, with a mission to empower individuals and communities through knowledge accessibility and digital transformation.
